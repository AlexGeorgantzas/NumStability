\vspace{6pt}
{\bfseries Result summary.}
For T1 and T2, L has lower mean elapsed time, observed-token lower bound, and submitted physical LOC. For T3, the tier means are close across conditions and show no consistent NumStability advantage. The task plots below retain their original per-task scales, so bar heights should be compared only within a panel.

{\small\bfseries Faster time, more tokens.} {\small Some T1 L runs finish sooner yet use more observed tokens because NumStability search and read outputs enlarge the context processed again on later model calls, including cached input, while theorem reuse shortens proof development and Lean checking.}
